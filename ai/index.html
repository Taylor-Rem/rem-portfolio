Questions

Common Questions

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can describe what you want in a text message, you can use everything I set up. The technical part is my job — that's what the install is.

What if the AI breaks something?

It can't break anything permanently. Every change is recorded before it goes live, and any change can be undone with one command. I'll show you the undo in our first session — it's the best part of the demo.

Do I actually own it?

Yes. Your accounts, your code, your data — and on the self-serve plans, your AI subscription too. Unlike all-in-one AI website startups, nothing I build holds your business hostage. The one exception is stated, not hidden: on Text Your Website, the texting service runs on my systems and my AI account. If we part ways you keep the site and everything in it; you'd just go back to making changes another way.

Why a monthly fee?

AI tools change monthly, and a setup that isn't maintained slowly stops being useful. On every plan the fee keeps your install current, includes a monthly health check with a written report, and gets you priority access when you need a hand. On the self-serve plans it doesn't cover making your routine changes — you do that by asking your AI, that's the product. On Text Your Website it does: the routine changes are what you're paying for, and I run the system that makes them.

Can I run the website AI myself?

Not as a package right now. There's one website plan — Text Your Website — and I run the compute: you install nothing, text a number, and the usage is included up to your allowance. If you want an AI on your own machine for your business generally, that's AI on Your Computer above; it just isn't wired to run your site for you.

What happens to what I text?

Your message and any photos go to my system, which uses an AI to make the change and reply. Photos you send for the site get published on the site, which is public. I don't sell or share any of it, and your number is never used for anything but replying to you. The details are in the privacy policy; texting terms are in the terms.
