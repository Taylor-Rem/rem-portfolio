Questions

Common Questions

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can describe what you want in a text message, you can use everything I set up. The technical part is my job — that's what the install is.

What if the AI breaks something?

It can't break anything permanently. Every change is recorded before it goes live, and any change can be undone with one command. I'll show you the undo in our first session — it's the best part of the demo.

Do I actually own it?

Yes — that's the whole point. Your accounts, your code, your data, your AI subscription. Unlike all-in-one AI website startups, nothing I build holds your business hostage. If we part ways, everything keeps working.

Why a monthly fee?

AI tools change monthly, and a setup that isn't maintained slowly stops being useful. The fee keeps your install current, includes a monthly health check with a written report, and gets you priority access when you need a hand.
