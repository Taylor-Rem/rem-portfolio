How It Works

Here's what working with me looks like — no jargon to learn, no developer to wait on.

1

Say it like you'd say it to a person — in Claude on your computer: "Draft the reply to this email in our voice," or "Turn this spreadsheet into a monthly report."

Questions

Common Questions

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can describe what you want in a text message, you can use everything I set up. The technical part is my job — that's what the install is.

What if the AI breaks something?

On your computer, Claude works inside the folders and rules we set, and I show you how to review and undo what it does in the first session. For a website changed by text, that's Patchlamp's job: every change is saved in the site's history and any change can be undone by asking.

Do I actually own it?

Yes. Your accounts, your code, your data, and your AI subscription. Unlike all-in-one AI startups, nothing I set up holds your business hostage: the install runs on your machine under your account, and the written house rules are yours to keep.

Why a monthly fee?

The care plan is optional. AI tools change monthly, and a setup that isn't maintained slowly stops being useful; $79 a month keeps your install current, with a monthly check-in and a written report. Skip it and everything still works — you just maintain it yourself.

I just want my website changed by text.

That's Patchlamp, not a session: pick a plan, pay, and you're texting Patch the same day. The install and the sessions are for the rest of your business — email, documents, spreadsheets, the work that isn't the website.
