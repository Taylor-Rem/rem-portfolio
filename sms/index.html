Taylor Remund · Text Your Website service

Clients on the text-message plan change their website by texting the service number: (937) 764-4699.

By texting this number you agree to receive SMS/MMS replies from Taylor Remund confirming your changes or asking a clarifying question. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out; reply HELP for help. Only numbers a client has authorized can use the service, and no messages are sent to anyone who has not texted first.

How it works

You text a request — add a show, change some wording, publish a photo — and the change is made to your website and put live. You get a text back when it's done, or a short question if something needs clarifying. That's the whole program: replies to what you send, nothing else.

Who can use it

The service only responds to phone numbers a client has authorized in writing as part of their service agreement. Texts from any other number are not acted on. The number is never used for marketing, and nobody is texted who hasn't texted first.

Stopping and restarting

Reply STOP to stop receiving messages. Because the service works by replying to you, stopping messages also stops the service acting on your requests — reply START to turn it back on. Reply HELP at any time, or email remundtaylor3@gmail.com.

Policies

How your number, messages, and photos are handled is in the Privacy Policy. The terms for using the service are in the Terms of Service.
