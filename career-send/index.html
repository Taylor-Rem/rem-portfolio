Career Send — Privacy

Effective November 13, 2026

Career Send is a small personal tool I run on my own computer. It sends email from my own Gmail account, in my name. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What it can access in my Google account

Only permission to send email on my behalf, plus my email address so the tool can confirm which account granted it. It cannot read, search, list, change or delete any email, contact or file.

What it keeps

The Google sign-in token, stored on my own computer and never shared. A log of each message it sends: the recipient, the subject, the date and the attached file. Nothing else from my Google account is collected or stored.

People who receive a message

The tool writes to people who published an email address in a public job posting, inviting contact. Messages are drafted with the help of an AI model (Claude, by Anthropic) from information about my own work and the public posting. The log above is kept on my computer. If you received a message and want it deleted or want no further contact, email me and I'll do both.

What I don't do with it

I don't sell or share any of this data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to anyone. Career Send's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The grant can be removed at any time from the Google account's third-party access settings, which stops the tool from sending anything.
