Messaging terms

Clients change their website or restaurant menu by texting a phone number I operate, or by messaging a bot on Telegram or Discord. These terms apply to all three. The SMS-specific consent language below applies to the phone number.

By texting the service number you consent to receive SMS and MMS messages from it in reply. Message frequency varies — you receive replies to the requests you send, plus occasional service notices. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out; reply HELP for help. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

  • Authorized senders only. The service responds to phone numbers and Telegram or Discord accounts the client has authorized. You are responsible for every request sent from a number or account you've authorized, including by people you've given access to. Tell me right away if one should be removed. Unauthorized senders get a refusal and no work is done.
  • Changes go live immediately. There is no preview step. A request from an authorized sender is carried out and published to your live website or menu, usually within a few minutes. Every change is recorded and can be undone — message "undo that" or ask me.
  • What the bot won't do. It works only inside the folder that holds your site and menu; anything outside it is blocked. Anything that needs an account, money, or a real backend (a domain, a store, a login, a form that emails someone, payments, refunds, customer data) is my work, not the bot's: it will tell you so and forward the request to me.
  • What I'll decline. I may decline any request that is outside the agreed scope, that would break the law or someone else's rights, that would publish content I reasonably consider harmful or misleading, or that would compromise the site's security. I'll tell you when that happens and why.
  • Availability. The service runs on equipment I operate. Today that is a machine in my office, on during my working hours (Mountain time) and off overnight; it is not a 24/7 service, and I don't guarantee any particular uptime or response time. Requests sent by SMS or Telegram while it's off are handled when it's back on; Discord messages sent while it's off need to be resent. If a request can't be completed, you'll be told rather than left waiting, and I'll be notified. Nothing about this affects your website itself, which is hosted independently and stays up regardless.
  • Limits. Each authorized sender has a daily cap on messages, and each project can have a monthly spending ceiling. Past either, the bot replies that the limit was reached and stops for the day; I'm notified. This protects your credit balance from a runaway.
  • Opting out stops the service. If you text STOP, the service can no longer reply to you and will not act on your requests until you text START.

Credits (managed tier)

On the managed tier the AI usage is paid for in prepaid credits, on top of the monthly fee. The rates are published on the services page: a message the bot handles is charged at three times what the AI usage cost me, rounded up to the nearest 25 cents, with a 50-cent minimum; a photo the AI generates is a flat $1 plus the message that asked for it; stock photos and photos you send are not charged beyond the message. Credits are sold in packs ($25 and $100), do not expire, and are non-refundable, including unused balance when the engagement ends. When your balance runs out the bot tells you and stops until you top up; your website stays up.

The usage ledger is the record. Every message and every generated photo is logged with its cost and your charge as it happens. That ledger is the authoritative record of what you were charged; your statement is generated from it, and you can ask to see your entries at any time. If you believe an entry is wrong, tell me within 30 days and I'll review it against the log of what the AI did.

Bring your own key. If you supply your own Anthropic API key, Anthropic bills you directly for the AI usage under your agreement with them, no credits are charged, and I charge a flat $15 per month relay fee on top of the monthly, invoiced. You're responsible for your key and for any spending limits on it.

Free tier. Restaurants on Plateful that connect their own AI assistant to their restaurant's account pay nothing to me for that access; their assistant's usage is between them and their assistant's provider, and Plateful's own terms and per-order pricing apply to the restaurant as usual.
