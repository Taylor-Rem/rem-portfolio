Patchlamp · Text your website

The lamp's on.

You text a number. Patch, the operator, opens your site's files, makes the change, checks it's live, and texts you back — usually in a minute. Text a photo and it's on the site. Text "undo that" and it's gone.

  • Nothing to install, no logins, no dashboard. Any phone, anyone you authorize.
  • Every change is saved in your site's history. It works inside a walled-off folder that holds only your things.
  • Anything Patch shouldn't handle by itself — a domain, a store, a login — it says so plainly and passes to Taylor.

from $99/mo · Standard $250/mo with a built site and Taylor on call · usage included
